Unit 2

Coming Closer to Christ

What I Want to Remember from This Unit:

"The plan of salvation was not a ‘backup plan.’ All has happened according to the perfect knowledge of an omnipotent God, even if we do not understand that at the time."

Context Summary

Lessons 3 and 4 examined Christ's role in the creation and redemption from the Fall, and His work through sacred covenants. Lesson 3 showed how Restoration scripture illuminates the creation and Fall in ways that biblical accounts do not, particularly that the Fall was part of God's plan from the beginning and that Christ was foreordained to atone for it. Lesson 4 explored the everlasting covenant that Christ has used throughout history to connect His children to His promises, culminating in the Abrahamic covenant and the latter-day gathering of Israel.

Christ as Creator and the Fall, Prompt 1

Please answer the following questions in your scripture journal: As you read about the creation of the earth from the various scriptural accounts, you learned some insights provided by Restoration scripture that were not given in the biblical accounts. Name one or two of these insights and why they are important to you. What did the Savior teach about the creation of marriage with Adam and Eve? What did He teach about the purpose of the Sabbath day? How does understanding these truths influence your feelings about the earth and about Jesus Christ?

Though this may not have been directly stated, I love the Latter-day Saint focus and emphasis on the purpose and symbolism behind the coat of skins being made through animal sacrifice in similitude of Christ. I also love that they are representative of the Temple Garment. I love how the Savior taught that marriage is ordained of God and that we are to cleave unto our spouses. I love that He clarified that the Sabbath was never meant to be a letter of the law to fulfill the specific list of things we need to do, but that we need to understand the spirit of the Sabbath and dedicate a day to the Lord, a day in which we go about doing good.

Christ as Creator and the Fall, Prompt 2

Comment on lessons you learn by pondering Jesus Christ as the Creator. You could share something based on the above reading and video or based on a different topic related to Christ as the Creator that stands out to you. Is there anything that you could apply in your life?

I have a strong testimony that Jesus Christ created this world. When I see the beauty of everyday things and I learn about science and how everything from the solar system down to the cells of our body had to work out perfectly the way they are for life on Earth to exist, I recognize that it was not a coincidence that we are here on this fully functioning and lively planet. I can apply these things by preparing to be a Creator myself by using my skills to be creative and make things that make the world a better place and make a difference.

Christ as Creator and the Fall, Prompt 3

Please answer the following question in your scripture journal: As you read about the Fall from the various scriptural accounts, what insights did you find in Restoration scripture that were not given in the biblical accounts? Share a few of these insights and why they are important to you.

The understanding that the Fall was part of God's plan is the truth that most separates our understanding of the Fall from that of other faiths'. Furthermore, Eve's later recognition and joy that she felt in having made the decision to partake of the fruit was not in a biblical account. But what I think is the most pivotal thing that Restoration scripture provides from the Fall is the understanding of how the Fall was a planned precursor to the Atonement of Jesus Christ. We understand that if Adam and Eve had not partaken of the fruit we would not be in a blissful perfect paradise right now, but rather still awaiting that decision. The Fall was not the backup plan after Adam and Eve did something that God could not have predicted or foreseen or prepared for. The Devil did not foil God's plan. Rather, God had prepared and chosen Christ since before the Earth was formed in order to suffer for the sins that God knew would come because of the Fall.

Covenants, Prompt 4

Compare the description of the covenant Christ made with Abraham in Genesis 12:1-3 and Abraham 2:3-11. What details of the covenant are added or clarified in the version found in the book of Abraham?

The Book of Abraham commands Abraham to take Lot with him out of the land of Haram to spread the gospel as ministers and they were in turn promised seed as an eternal possession. Abraham was told he would be a blessing and bring the Priesthood to his family and that all that obey God will be part of his family, he also promises eternal life.

Temple blessings provide me with peace. They bring me joy and calm that is unlike anything the world has to offer. It helps me feel close to God and feel connected with His children on the other side of the veil. It allows me the peace of mind of knowing that my family will be forever.

Covenants, Prompt 6

Imagine someone asked you why the four Gospels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John) describe Jesus preaching only to Jews. If Jesus loves everyone, why wasn't He also preaching in Athens, or Rome, or Alexandria? In your journal, explain the answer you would give.

I would tell that individual that the four Gospels describe Jesus preaching only to the Jews not because He only loved the Jews but because it wasn't the Lord's timing. When His apostles asked, Christ said that He first needed to gather His lost sheep of Israel. Often the Lord needs to prepare His messengers before sending them to spread the word. In this case, Christ was preparing Israelites to later go and declare the gospel unto the Gentiles. It was simply a matter of the Lord's timing, but He had always planned to declare the gospel unto all children of God.

Covenants, Prompt 8

As you watch the video, ask yourself, “How does this video portray people fulfilling the mission of the Abrahamic covenant? How can I participate in the Abrahamic covenant in similar ways?”

Although it does not mention Abraham, Israel, or Jews. There are no missionaries with black name tags, and no one visits a temple. No one mentions Isaiah or preaches about Jesus, this video is still a portrayal of people fulfilling the mission of the Abrahamic covenant because they are gathering together and making people feel welcome through deliberate and selfless forms of service. By sharing what they have and inviting others that are less fortunate to join them they are gathering Israel. Helping fix the car is also a service that brings them together.
