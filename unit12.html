Unit 12

Coming Closer to Christ

What I Want to Remember from This Unit:

"The scriptures say the disciples delighted in having the opportunity to be crucified for Christ's sake after boldly testifying of His divinity. When was the last time I stood up for what I believe, despite my beliefs being unpopular?"

Context Summary

Lesson 20 studied how the risen Christ continued to guide His Church through the Apostles and the Holy Ghost in the early Christian community, drawing from the book of Acts and the testimonies of Peter and John. Lesson 21 examined Paul's teachings on Christ's Resurrection and the gospel of salvation, focusing on key passages in Corinthians, Galatians, and Ephesians that describe what it means to live by faith, be led by the Spirit, and wear the whole armor of God.

Key Scriptures

  • Acts 1:1-8; 2:1-47
  • Acts 9:1-30
  • 2 Peter 1:15-17; 1 John 1:1-5
  • 1 Corinthians 15:1-58
  • 2 Nephi 9:6-13
  • Galatians 2:16-21; 3:1-29
  • Philippians 2:5-11
  • Romans 5-8

Full Journal Submission

Christ and the Early Church, Prompt 1

Having read the testimonies of Peter and John, briefly reflect on your own testimony of Jesus by writing a short journal entry wherein you reflect on the basis of your own testimony of the risen Savior.

Unlike the apostles, my witness of Jesus Christ was not physically being with Him on the Earth or seeing His resurrection. My witness was from life experience of many small moments when I felt His presence though I could not see Him there. It is like that talk called “Rays of Light.” I have had so many rays of light through living the gospel and studying it. I have felt the Spirit so much, so profoundly, that I cannot deny Christ lives and is real. I have been so blessed by God that I know He lives. My testimony comes from the Holy Spirit.

Christ and the Early Church, Prompt 2

After watching this section, identify the six components of Peter's discourse. In a paragraph, identify the key points of his discourse.

The first point is saying that it was the power of God that healed him. That Christ is the Son of God, betrayed and crucified. He has power to heal the faithful through the name of Christ. We need to repent and be converted unto the Lord. Jesus Christ will return. He will be received until the time of the restitution of all things. He claims that Christ was prophesied of by Moses. He speaks of the covenant of Abraham and the Israelites as inheritors of it.

Christ and the Early Church, Prompt 3

Ponder on the response of Peter and John when they were threatened and ordered to stop preaching the word of God. Have you ever faced threats, harassment, or ridicule for the sake of the gospel? What did you do? How did this reflect Peter's example? Or, what could you have done differently to better emulate Peter's example? How will you react in such situations in the future?

Peter's boldness and bravery in declaring the truth of the gospel is very respectable. It is even inspiring. I felt the Spirit as I watched that video. It motivates me to be more bold in my declaration of the gospel and the truth and to be less afraid and apologetic. I have had moments in my life where I have felt the Spirit come over me and help me to boldly declare the gospel. I know it cannot come from me because my natural man is the opposite of bold, and many times that natural man has won. However, I do know that I have never once regretted speaking boldly of Jesus Christ and His gospel. I desire to stay true to that and follow all promptings that I have to share the gospel.

Paul's Teachings on Jesus Christ, Prompt 1

How has your faith in the Resurrection affected how you live and the decisions you make? Why would our preaching and faith be in vain if Christ had not risen from the dead?

Our preaching would be in vain if Christ had not risen because there would be no afterlife. There would be no eternity. There would be no purpose to what we are doing and living and preaching the gospel except to improve our temporal experiences in life. This knowledge of the Resurrection motivates me to make good decisions and want to share this news with others.

Paul's Teachings on Jesus Christ, Prompt 2

Consider the false doctrine Paul identified in 1 Corinthians 15:12. Then, using 2 Nephi 9:6-9, make a list of what would happen to all humankind if there were no Resurrection. Using phrases from 1 Corinthians 15:54-57 and 2 Nephi 9:10-13, write a brief paragraph that expresses your feelings about the Resurrection.

If there were no resurrection of the dead, “this flesh must have laid down to rot and to crumble to its mother earth, to rise no more.” Furthermore, “corruption could not put on incorruption,” and we would die with no redemption and no afterlife. Luckily, Christ did rise from the dead, and fortunately that means that we too will rise from the dead. This provides great comfort regarding death and regarding lost loved ones. As the apostle Paul said, “thanks be to God, which giveth us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.”

Paul's Teachings on Jesus Christ, Prompt 3

Ponder Galatians 2:20. Write a short paragraph about the ways you have experienced the meaning of this verse in your life.

This verse is very powerful to me. As I read it I could feel Paul's devotion and conversion to Jesus Christ. He truly loved the Lord and wanted to live for Him and for Him alone. He was a true disciple by the means of it. I want to be like that and live for Christ with every breath, minute, and moment.

Paul's Teachings on Jesus Christ, Prompt 4

Think about your private prayers, scripture study, church attendance, service to others, and other religious practices. What does it mean for you to be led by the Spirit in these practices rather than having your actions dictated by laws? See Galatians 3:2-5.

As I learned about Paul's teachings about being saved by faith rather than works, it caused me to reflect on my life and motivations. Do I choose to read scriptures, take this class in the summer, pray, obey commandments, etc. because of the law or because of my faith in and love for Christ? I want to be obedient due to love, but sometimes these things can become habits and customs that we don't think of the purpose behind them. I want to focus on doing these as Spirit-led actions.

Paul's Teachings on Jesus Christ, Prompt 5

Briefly outline the purpose of the law of Moses according to Paul.

According to Paul, the purpose of the Law of Moses was preparatory. It was like a schoolmaster that prepared people to learn and live by faith, so that Jesus can save us. He is simply teaching that faith was always a precursor to the law and that the law merely pointed us to Christ.

Paul's Teachings on Jesus Christ, Prompt 6

In a short paragraph, describe how you have felt godly sorrow in your life and how this has helped you draw closer to Christ and become a better disciple.

Godly sorrow has helped me grow closer to Christ and be a better disciple because it has drawn me to repentance. When I have felt this, I have been motivated to get rid of the feeling and thus follow the steps of repentance to salvation and receive joy. As I have done so I have felt peace that can only come from the Spirit, and the feeling of the Spirit is better than any feeling that can come from sin.

Paul's Teachings on Jesus Christ, Prompt 7

As you consider the different pieces of the armor of God (Ephesians 6:14-18), which piece do you feel is most critical for your life right now? What could you do to make sure that you are spiritually protected so you can “withstand in the evil day”? (Ephesians 6:13).

I believe that the sword of the Spirit is the most critical in my life right now. I believe that I need to further prioritize and commit my whole soul and more of my time to the Lord. As I obey the commandments and repent, and study God more and testify of Him, I can feel His Spirit more. As President Nelson said, we will need the Spirit constantly to survive spiritually. I desire this.
