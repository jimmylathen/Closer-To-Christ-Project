Unit 13

Coming Closer to Christ

What I Want to Remember from This Unit:

"The Old Testament Jehovah is the New Testament Messiah. Christ fulfills ancient prophecy. The Law of Moses is full of symbolism about Jesus Christ."

Context Summary

Lesson 22 examined early Christian teachings about Jesus's death as an atoning sacrifice and His role as the Great High Priest who mediates and intercedes for humanity. Drawing on the symbolism of the Jerusalem Temple, the Mosaic sacrificial system, and New Testament passages, particularly from Hebrews, the lesson traced how Christ fulfilled and transcended the priestly and sacrificial roles of the Old Testament.

Key Scriptures

  • John 1:35-36; John 19:13-37
  • Romans 3:23-26; Romans 5:8-11
  • Hebrews 2:17-18; 4:14-16
  • Hebrews 10:1-22
  • Revelation 1:1-20; 5:6
  • Mark 15:25-39; Matthew 27:45-51; Luke 23:44-46

Full Journal Submission

Jesus as the Atoning Sacrifice and Great High Priest, Prompt 1

Briefly write some personal reflections on what these New Testament teachings mean to you and how they affect the way you view the significance of Jesus's death in your own life.

I find these teachings to be truly fascinating. It is so interesting to learn about the way in which Judaism evolved into Christianity and the symbolic significance of it all. It is amazing the amount of parallels between Christ and the Old Testament, and it truly increases my faith that He was and is the long-awaited Messiah.

Jesus as the Atoning Sacrifice and Great High Priest, Prompt 2

Briefly write some personal reflections on what these New Testament teachings mean to you and how they affect the way you view the significance of Jesus's role as your mediator and intercessor.

Seeing Christ as the great mediator that tore down the veil through His prayer at the exact moment that the priests were at the Holy of Holies is fascinating. I love the symbolism that Christ is our intermediary that will plead our case and that connects us with Deity. It reminds me of why we pray always in Christ's name and gives me more background and understanding for that truth.
