Unit 6

Coming Closer to Christ

What I Want to Remember from This Unit:

"Christ universally represents victory over physical and spiritual death to all believers. However, like the apostles, our unique life experiences and perspectives may change what we highlight about that or what it means to us, as Christ is able to fulfill all of our spiritual needs as He ministers to us through His Spirit. Christ loves the marginalized, He fulfills Old Testament prophecy, He is 100% human, and He is 100% God."

Context Summary

Lesson 10 examined how Matthew, Luke, and John each portray the birth and infancy of Jesus from a distinct perspective, with Matthew emphasizing His connection to King David and Old Testament prophecy, Luke focusing on Jesus as a universal Savior who cares for the poor and marginalized, and John presenting Him as the eternal Word who condescended to take on mortal life. Lesson 11 turned to the beginning of Jesus's mortal ministry, covering His baptism, temptations in the wilderness, and proclamation of the Kingdom of God. Together, these lessons showed how Christ's earliest experiences in mortality were foundational to His identity and mission as the Savior of all.

Full Journal Submission

The Condescension, Birth, and Infancy of Jesus, Prompt 1

Briefly write some personal reflections on ways in which Matthew's depiction of Jesus's birth helps you to see Jesus differently and how this depiction might have application to your own life.

After learning about the kind of Messiah that the Jews were expecting, I kind of wondered to myself which prophecies of the Old Testament Jesus fulfilled during His earthly ministry. Matthew does a wonderful job in the first two chapters already making five connections between the Old Testament and Christ's early life. His understanding of scripture and Christ's relation to it in connection is amazing. It reminds me of when Christ Himself read scriptures to the disciples, showing His connection to them after His resurrection.

The Condescension, Birth, and Infancy of Jesus, Prompt 2

Briefly write some personal reflections on ways in which Luke's depiction of Jesus's birth helps you to see Jesus differently and how this depiction might have application to your own life.

I love Luke's take on Jesus. The idea that Christ is for all people, especially the marginalized, just emphasizes His boldness and bravery while maintaining His humility and meekness. He was brave to break boundaries and meek and humble in His outreach to the ostracized. I also love His emphasis on Mary and how she got help.

The Condescension, Birth, and Infancy of Jesus, Prompt 3

Write some personal reflections on ways in which John's depiction of Jesus's condescension helps you to see Jesus differently and how this depiction might have application to your own life.

This condescension of Jesus is a very valuable image of Christ. Understanding how logos served as a mediator between God and people helps me understand Jehovah being Christ in the Old Testament. It also amplifies the reality of Christ's divinity.

Beginning of Ministry, Prompt 1

Based on the insights provided in this assignment, briefly write some of your personal reflections on what it means to you to know that the baptism was a moment of revelation to Jesus concerning His identity and mission. What does this insight mean to you personally?

It is fascinating to know that Jesus Himself had to learn and grow “grace to grace.” When I think of that phrase I'm always interested because I know Jesus is the one that brought grace to us through the Atonement. I may be thinking too far into this, but I wonder if Jesus was benefitted by His own Atonement retroactively as He grew in light and knowledge in His youth.

Beginning of Ministry, Prompt 2

After reading the accounts of Jesus's temptations in the wilderness and considering the doctrinal significance of His need to experience temptation, briefly record some personal reflections on how this affects your understanding of Jesus and His role as your Savior, as well as what these insights could mean for you personally.

This understanding that Jesus not only suffered for my sins but also my pains, afflictions, and even my temptations changes my understanding of the Atonement of Jesus Christ completely. He's not just there as a safety net for when I screw up, but He knows how temptations feel and thus can have mercy on us and help us overcome them. He's not only the way to be forgiven but also the grace that allows us to change and overcome our mistakes and bad habits.
