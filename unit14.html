Unit 14

Coming Closer to Christ

What I Want to Remember from This Unit:

"Christ's earthly ministry fulfilled much of Old Testament prophecy. His glorious Second Coming, which will cover the entire earth, will fulfill the rest. The ongoing Restoration and coming forth of the Book of Mormon testify that the kingdom of God is at hand."

Context Summary

Lesson 23 explored the Second Coming of Jesus Christ as described in Matthew 24, the epistles of Paul, and the book of Revelation, examining the signs that precede it, the purposes it will fulfill, and how believers should prepare. Lesson 24 examined the Restoration of the gospel through Joseph Smith, beginning with the First Vision and continuing through additional visions and scripture, showing how Christ remains personally involved in guiding His Church in the latter days.

Key Scriptures

  • Matthew 24; JS-Matthew 1
  • Matthew 25:1-46
  • 1 Thessalonians 4:13-5:12
  • Revelation 1:1-20; 4-11; 19
  • D&C 76:11-24
  • D&C 110:1-10
  • D&C 122:5-8
  • "The Living Christ"

Full Journal Submission

Second Coming of Christ, Prompt 1

As you read these verses, identify four conditions about which Jesus prophetically warns.

Wars, rumors of wars, pestilences, famines, earthquakes, false Christs.

Second Coming of Christ, Prompt 2

As you read this section of the discourse, answer the following questions: What similarities do you notice between what Jesus tells the disciples here and what He previously said to them? What does Jesus say here about His Second Coming? What is the purpose of the parable of the fig tree? What does Jesus say about the preaching of the gospel? Why does Jesus compare the “days of Noah” with those immediately preceding the Second Coming?

Similarities I see are that in both cases Christ warns the apostles of false Christs and false prophets coming in the last days. Christ states that His coming will not be in private in a secret or secluded room or conference, but that His Second Coming will not be meek and humble like the first. Rather, it will cover the whole Earth. The parable shows that these signs indicate the Second Coming is near, like how leaves predict summer. However, like summer, there is no exact moment everyone knows the Second Coming will occur. The gospel will be taught to all the world so that people can accept it or deny it and leave to choose wickedness in preparation for the Second Coming. The days of Noah are like the Second Coming: Noah's Earth was covered with water in a baptism by water, and at the Second Coming the Earth will be metaphorically and somewhat literally baptized by fire.

Second Coming of Christ, Prompt 3

In a short paragraph, outline a plan to better develop and use one of the talents or spiritual gifts the Lord has given to you.

I plan to better develop my spiritual gift of discernment by prioritizing my relationship with the Holy Ghost. As I strive to learn more about the gospel through divinely appointed sources, I can discern truth and share that truth with others. I can use the Spirit of discernment to make the right decision because I know it is right.

Second Coming of Christ, Prompt 4

As you read, answer the following questions: What are some specific things Paul says to the Thessalonian saints about the Second Coming in 1 Thessalonians 5:1-8? What can you do to invite greater spiritual light into your life? How can a proper understanding of the Second Coming of Jesus Christ bring greater peace, hope, and joy to our daily living?

In verses 1-8 Paul states that we must put on the armor of God to prepare for the Second Coming. He states that the righteous that prepare for the Second Coming need not worry or fear. I can invite greater spiritual light into my life by staying virtuous and charitable to others with an eye single to God's glory. Understanding the Second Coming of Jesus Christ brings greater hope, peace, and joy to our daily living because it brings us to understand we have a perfect God. Unlike what the Romans or Greeks believed, we can trust that our God is just and merciful.

Second Coming of Christ, Prompt 5

As you read these verses, keep the following questions in mind: Of the words and phrases used in Revelation 1:5-8 to describe Jesus Christ, which ones are the most meaningful to you? How do these verses work together to reveal the Savior's identity, character, and eternal mission? What do the symbolic descriptions of Jesus Christ in Revelation 1:13-18 teach about His attributes and His work?

This shows Christ's compassion on us and His mission to bless us to be kings and priests and reach our divine potential, Him being the mediator. This teaches that His work and teachings and church on the last day will be “complete,” as symbolized by the number 7.

Second Coming of Christ, Prompt 6

Answer the following questions: What do you need to do so that the description of the righteous in Revelation 7:14 can apply to you? What are you doing to distance yourself from sins that will be prevalent prior to the Second Coming? What more could you do to “stand in holy places”? (Doctrine and Covenants 45:32). Though there is much in these chapters in Revelation that may cause some to feel anxiety, what in these chapters gives you hope and confidence for the future?

In order to be one of the righteous I have to accept Christ's Atonement through faith on His name and repentance. To stand in holy places I need to be in the world without being of the world. I must repent of my sins and endure faithfully until the end. The hope is that Jesus Christ is just as merciful as He is just. He loves and cares about us, and if we are making honest efforts to be good and sanctified and doing as He asks, He will support us through His grace.

Second Coming of Christ, Prompt 7

As you consider and ponder the great things that are revealed in Revelation as it pertains to the Second Coming and the ultimate destiny of this earth, consider the following: Why was it important for the Saints in John's day to know the end of the story? Why is it important for us? How can knowing of the eventual triumph of Christ help you in the struggles of your daily life?

Knowing the end of the story allows the saints in John's day to have faith that Christ will conquer sin in the end and they will rise with Him in the Second Coming if they die in faith. It is important for us for the same reason, but also for us to know there's no saying when that day will come, so we need to be prepared always. This helps us know that while we may struggle with evil in mortality, it eventually will be defeated and punished for good by God.

Restoration, Prompt 1

What have the accounts of Joseph Smith's First Vision added to your understanding of Jesus from the biblical material we studied earlier in the course?

The accounts of Joseph Smith's First Vision have helped me so much to understand the nature of the Godhead. I know that Jesus Christ and Heavenly Father have bodies and are resurrected and divine beings due to the First Vision. As I relayed Joseph's story of the First Vision during my mission, I felt the Spirit powerfully and knew it to be true.

Restoration, Prompt 2

What do these visions teach you about Jesus Christ? Once you've written your response, compare your thoughts with those below.

The main thing I took away from these scriptures is how much God cares about how we treat each other. He went over the commandments, emphasizing that we must obey the commandments so we can love our neighbor. I love how personally involved Christ was in the early Church, and it helps me remember that He cares just as much about us now and is just as involved, simply with a larger Church and more servants than they initially had.

Restoration, Prompt 3

Which phrases from Jesus Christ stood out most to you? Why did you find these to be significant?

I love that Christ does not just talk the talk but He also walks the walk. When we're suffering, we can never tell the Lord that He doesn't understand us or has never gone through what we are going through. He has suffered all that we suffer, and incomprehensibly more, for the pain, suffering, and afflictions of all men. I understood this as I read Him say that He has descended below it all, which He quite literally has.

Restoration, Prompt 4

Which phrases stood out most to you from your reading of The Living Christ? Why did you find these to be significant?

I love the boldness of the testimony of the apostles in The Living Christ, and I love that they quoted Joseph, stating that they knew Him and had seen Him. This is a testament to a living Church in which the modern-day apostles likely have similar experiences, and that scripture is even more significant to them.

Restoration, Prompt 5

Two years from now, what do you think you will remember from this course? How do you think your life will be better because you took this course?

I believe that I will remember the way in which there are so many witnesses of Christ and that His character is so perfect. That He is just and merciful, loves us, and wants us to return to Him. I will remember that differences between witnesses only provide new perspectives rather than contradictions.

Restoration, Prompt 6

What have you learned about Jesus Christ this semester that has been most meaningful to you?

I have learned that Christ cares about and loves us more than we are capable of understanding. His virtue and strength are not only admirable but what we need to strive to emulate.

Restoration, Prompt 7

In what ways (small or large) have you changed your life this semester because you have studied the life of Jesus Christ?

I have made changes by striving to find Christ in the scriptures and focusing on Him. I have gained a deeper respect and love for the New Testament's depiction of Christ. I have tried to apply His teachings and make the changes in my life that I knew I needed to make.

Restoration, Prompt 8

What will you do in your life moving forward to learn more about Jesus Christ and come closer to Him?

I want to read Jesus the Christ, I want to keep reading the Bible in my scripture study rather than just the Book of Mormon. I desire to read the Gospels in a synoptic study.
