Unit 1

Coming Closer to Christ

What I Want to Remember from This Unit:

"There is always hope in Jesus Christ. Through His grace, the Holy Ghost will prompt us to repent, His grace will help us be able to repent, and His Atoning Sacrifice will ultimately cleanse us of our sins."

Context Summary

Lesson 1 centered on Jesus Christ as the heart of the plan of salvation, examining the meaning of His name and scriptural titles and showing how His Atonement connects every stage of eternal life from premortal existence through the kingdoms of glory. Lesson 2 explored the many ways we can "learn of" Christ, including through scriptures, Church leaders, art, scholarship, and personal spiritual experience, and introduced principles of exegesis and eisegesis to help students interpret and apply scripture more meaningfully. Together, these lessons invited a deeper kind of discipleship rooted not just in knowing facts about Christ, but in actively seeking to know Him as a person.

Full Journal Submission

Jesus Christ, Prompt 1

What does Jesus Christ mean to you?

To me, Jesus Christ means hope. He is both the way that I can be forgiven for my sins but also the way in which I can bring myself to repentance and make the changes that I need. Through His grace there is hope that my life can improve.

Jesus Christ, Prompt 2

Imagine your friend asks you to explain what the name "Jesus Christ" means and why this meaning is significant to you.

The name Jesus Christ has a very significant meaning. Jesus comes from the name Joshua, which was actually a common name at the time and refers to the salvation we can receive through Jesus Christ. Christ is not a last name, but is a title to describe Christ's role as the Son of God being the "Anointed One." These names are significant to me because they remind me that Christ is the chosen Son of God sent to save me from my sins.

Jesus Christ, Prompt 3

What did you learn from each of these scriptures about the name of Jesus Christ?

Through these scriptures, I understood with deeper meaning the importance and power of Christ's name. I recognized the way in which Jesus' name is the only one that will save us at the last day. I learned that in our successes and joys we need to recognize and praise Christ's name and hand in doing so. I learned that we must perform all our labors in His name.

Jesus Christ, Prompt 4

List two or three insights you gained from Oaks's "Taking upon Us the Name of Christ."

From President Oaks' talk I learned many important things. Such as the fact that the name of Christ can refer to places in which the Priesthood is used such as temples. I also gained a deeper understanding of what it means to take upon ourselves the name of Christ and know the name by which we are called.

Jesus Christ, Prompt 5

As you read President Nelson's talk, which title of Christ was most meaningful to you and why?

I love the name Creator as it is described as "Divine Expression" because it helps me to think of the beauty of all God's creations as divine expressions of deity. Yesterday, I was watching the Bee Movie and when I saw the impact that such an insignificant-seeming insect has on the Earth and its beauty and ability to be self-sustainable, I contemplated on the intricacies and details that Jesus and our Heavenly Father considered in the creation of every little thing.

Jesus Christ, Prompt 6

What do these scriptures teach about Jesus Christ and His role in the plan of salvation?

These scriptures demonstrate Christ's intimate involvement with us in each and every step of the Plan of Salvation. The scriptures describe His care in creation, the grace He provides during our life on Earth, and His role in the Spirit World. The later scriptures describe His resurrection and our ability to be resurrected due to His, as well as the judgment and His advocacy for us, and ultimately His position in the Kingdoms of Glory.

Learn of Me, Prompt 2

What sources of information have helped you learn about Jesus Christ in your life so far? What sources have played the greatest role?

The Holy Ghost has played the greatest role in helping me learn about Jesus. Whether it is the feeling that I have received that is far greater than any other feeling as I have read the scriptures about Christ, testified of Christ before, on, and after my mission, or as I have listened to others testify of Christ during church talks, I am so grateful for the Spirit that I have felt that has shown me that these things are true. My knowledge and testimony of Christ has come through the Holy Ghost as I have sought Him through divinely appointed sources.

Learn of Me, Prompt 3

What does Burnand's painting make you think about or feel? Jesus is not depicted, but what does the painting communicate about Him?

The apostles look like they are in shock. Both Peter and John appear to be anxious anticipating if the rumors that Christ has risen are true, seemingly wanting to avoid getting their hopes up and then being disappointed if it were not true.

Learn of Me, Prompt 4

After watching Bednar and Cardall's "One by One," what doctrine about the Savior does this song teach? How did it make you feel?

The song "One by One" made me recognize how the Lord loves all and loves each as we are supposed to. I felt that He has a special role and calling for each of us. I had the feeling that we are asked to minister to the one and show them God's love. It teaches the same doctrine Christ taught when He mentioned leaving the 99 to go after the lost sheep. He cares and loves us individually, and there is a constant and deliberate effort to bring each of us home to Him.

Learn of Me, Prompt 5

What are your impressions of the film The Christ Child? What does it teach about Jesus Christ, and what feelings does it invoke?

I have always loved this video. I really appreciate the realistic depiction of the Nativity. It makes me feel like I really am there. The video teaches that Christ, even from a young age, was recognized by some as the Savior of the World. It teaches that He came from humble beginnings. The feelings the film invokes are feelings of love and respect. Especially seeing Him depicted as a toddler brings me peace and a new perspective on His innocence, and in some way makes me feel more appreciative of His suffering for me.
